WHY THESE WORDS ARE USED
The population, treatment contrasts, and endpoint come from the verified Stage 2
evidence objects:

- Residential treatment: long-term versus short-term residential setting.
- Outpatient treatment: intensive versus non-intensive outpatient setting.
- Outcome: recorded administrative completion (REASON=1).

The policy-tree variable terms come from the verified Stage 2 evidence bundle:

- Residential tree: race, additional substances, and primary route of
  administration, including oral and inhalation categories.
- Outpatient tree: referral source, additional substances, employment status,
  and primary substance, including methamphetamine or speed.

Completion, retention, dropout, attrition, and discharge are retrieval synonyms
for treatment continuation and administrative exit. Alternative spellings such
as non-intensive/nonintensive and self-referral/self referral prevent avoidable
retrieval loss. The added terms broaden literature retrieval; they are not new
variables or empirical findings from this study.

HOW TO EXPORT THE RESULTS
Export article metadata and abstracts; full-text PDFs are not required.

For each search:
1. Select Export > Tab-delimited file.
2. Do not select the checkbox for the first 50 visible records. That checkbox
   selects only the current page.
3. In the export dialog, select Records from 1 through the total result count.
4. Select Full Record and Cited References.
5. Select UTF-8 encoding.
6. Save in this S4 folder using exactly these names:

WoS_residential_20260926.txt
WoS_outpatient_20260926.txt

If Web of Science requires export batches, use:

WoS_residential_20260926_part001.txt
WoS_residential_20260926_part002.txt
WoS_outpatient_20260926_part001.txt
WoS_outpatient_20260926_part002.txt

Standard full-record exports are limited to 1,000 records at a time. For more
than 1,000 results, export 1-1000, 1001-2000, and so on. Do not use Fast 5000:
it omits fields needed by S4, including full abstracts in some exports.

Keep the result count, search date, database, exact query, and export filenames
in the search record. Batch files must be merged with one header row before the
current S4 notebook can read them.
